1. Overview

Remote Mouse Suite ("the app", "we") turns your phone into a wireless trackpad, keyboard, and TV remote for devices on your own network. This policy explains what data the app and its third-party services collect, why, and your choices.

We designed the app to keep your control traffic local: commands you send to your computer or TV go directly to that device on your network and are not routed through or stored on our servers. We do not operate user accounts and do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Information collected

a) Data you provide directly

  • None required. The app has no sign-up or login. You do not create an account or give us personal details to use it.

b) Data collected automatically by third-party services

This version uses Google Firebase for diagnostics and reliability. It does not use any advertising SDK and does not collect an advertising identifier.

DataServicePurpose
App usage & diagnostics (e.g. app opens, which features are used, connection success/failure, in aggregate)Google Firebase AnalyticsUnderstand and improve the app
Crash reports & performance data (stack traces, device model, OS version)Google Firebase CrashlyticsDiagnose and fix crashes
Push-notification tokenGoogle Firebase Cloud MessagingDeliver notifications (only if notifications are enabled)

We receive analytics and crash data in aggregated/diagnostic form. Firebase is configured by the app at build time; if a build ships without Firebase configured, none of the above is collected.

c) Data kept only on your device

  • Pairing credentials / connection settings (the token used to authenticate with your own computer) are stored in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and never leave your device or get sent to us.

3. How we use information

  • Operate core features (connect to and control your computer/TV).
  • Diagnose crashes and improve reliability and features.

We do not sell your personal information. This version shows no ads and does not use your data for advertising.

4. Advertising and in-app purchases

This version does not show ads and has no in-app purchases. No advertising SDK is active, no advertising identifier is used, and no consent (UMP) ad prompt is shown because the app never requests ads.

If a future version reintroduces advertising, it will be clearly ad-supported, this policy and the store listing will be updated, and — where required by law (e.g. the EEA/UK under GDPR) — a Google-certified consent prompt will be shown before any ad is requested. See Appendix A for the disclosures that would apply in that case.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under the age required by your local law. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will address it.

6. Third-party services

These providers process data as described above under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google general privacy policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy

(Advertising and billing providers are listed in Appendix A and are not used by this version.)

7. Data sharing and transfer

We do not share your personal data with third parties except the service providers above, who process it to provide their functions to us, and except where required by law. Data handled by Google may be processed on servers outside your country under Google's safeguards.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data (pairing credentials, settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Analytics and crash data held by Google are retained per Google's retention settings.
  • To request deletion of data associated with you, contact us at the email above and we will act on it consistent with the providers' tooling and applicable law.

9. Security

Data sent to Google's services is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Local control traffic to your own computer/TV stays on your network; for that reason we recommend using the app on your own trusted Wi-Fi network rather than open/public networks. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data. To exercise these, contact us at the email above.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will change the effective date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app or on this page.
