What we never had in the first place

Health calculations (BMI, body fat, calories) and financial calculations (loan amounts, interest, salary) are never uploaded. Those figures stay on your device even while you are signed in, so there is nothing on our servers to delete.

What we keep, and why

  • Deleted history is tombstoned for 30 days before it is removed from our servers for good. That is what stops a deletion being undone by an old device that has not synced in a while.
  • Diagnostics collected by the advertising SDK are held by Google under Google's own retention policies, not ours. We operate no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • Records we are legally required to retain — kept only as long as the law requires, then deleted.

Advertising identifiers are controlled by your device, not by us. You can reset or delete yours in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and you can change your personalised-ads choice any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options.
