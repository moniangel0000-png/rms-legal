Privacy Policy

All in One Calculator, published by Akhuns Tech.

Last updated: 10 August 2026

The short version

  • Every calculator works without an account. Signing in is optional and only exists to sync your calculation history between your own devices.
  • Your health and financial figures never leave your device. Weights, heights, calorie and pregnancy dates, loan amounts, salaries — these are stored locally and are never uploaded, even when you are signed in.
  • The app shows ads, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects advertising identifiers and approximate location. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your answer at any time.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app or from the web, without reinstalling.

What is collected

Advertising — Google Mobile Ads SDK

The app displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. The Ads SDK collects and shares the following with Google and its advertising partners:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set IDAdvertising and marketing
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressAdvertising and marketing
App interactions, such as launches and tapsAdvertising and marketing
Diagnostics and crash informationAnalytics and app functionality

This is shared data, not merely collected: it is transferred to Google and to advertising partners. It is required for the ad-supported version of the app to function.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting any personalised advertising, using Google's consent management platform. If you do not consent, the app does not request ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Android settings, under Privacy → Ads.

Optional account — Firebase Authentication

If — and only if — you choose to create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, a user ID, and technical information including your IP address and user agent. This is used for account management only. It is not shared with third parties for advertising.

Sign-in is entirely optional. Every calculator works without it.

Optional sync — Cloud Firestore

While you are signed in, the app can sync your calculation history so it follows you between your own devices. A synced entry contains the expression, its result, and timestamps.

What is deliberately excluded from sync

Calculations from the health and financial categories are never uploaded. That covers BMI, body fat, calorie and water intake, pregnancy dates, age and date calculations, loan and EMI figures, interest, salary, overtime, work hours, savings, profit margins, tax and currency amounts.

This is enforced in three independent places rather than by policy alone: the app filters these records before any upload; it also refuses to store them if they somehow arrive from the server; and the server's own security rules reject them by category. Consequently the app declares no collection of health and fitness data and no collection of financial information.

Synced data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. If you use the app from outside India, your synced history is transferred to and stored in India.

Currency exchange rates

The currency converter fetches published exchange rates through a proxy operated for this app. That request contains a currency code and nothing else — no amount, no account, no device identifier — and the conversion is performed on your device. The rate provider therefore never learns what you converted or how much.

What is not collected

  • No health or fitness data. Health calculator inputs stay on your device.
  • No financial information. Loan, salary and tax inputs stay on your device.
  • No contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, files or precise location. The app does not request these permissions. It holds only internet and network-state permissions, plus the advertising ID permission added by the Ads SDK.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are not enabled in this app.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you clear your history or uninstall the app.
  • Synced history: until you delete it or delete your account. Deleted entries are marked as deleted and removed from the server after 30 days, which is what stops a deletion from being undone by an old device that has not synced recently.
  • Account records: removed when you delete your account.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's own policies, not ours. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Deleting your data

Two routes, both of which delete the same things:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account and data.
  • On the web, without reinstalling: see the account deletion page.

Deleting your account removes your synced history and your account record, and clears the app's local data on that device.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time — for advertising, through Settings → Privacy options. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a resident of a US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have the right to know what is collected, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. The in-app privacy options control the advertising choice.

To exercise any of these, use the deletion routes above or email us.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Synced data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure, and the server rules restrict every record to the account that created it — no account can read another account's data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page is updated and, where the change affects what is collected, the Data safety information on the Google Play listing is updated in the same release.
